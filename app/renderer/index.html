Scores come from the computer analysis once it has run; before that, from what the engine that played the move reported (shown in italics). Scores are from White's side.

UCI engines for playing and analysis. Add an engine by choosing its program file; Options sets its own settings, such as threads and hash size; Command line sets options it is started with.

Turn analysis on with the Analysis button in the toolbar. It follows the position on the board, including when you step back through the moves or browse games from a file, and uses its own copy of the engine. It pauses while an engine is playing a game.

The AI model used for AI annotate (under Annotate in the Moves tab) and the AI panel's chat. It is reached through an OpenAI-compatible API: a local server such as oMLX, LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp or vLLM, or a hosted one.

Nothing is sent until you press AI annotate or send a message in the AI panel: then the game on the board goes to this endpoint, as PGN, with the position shown. It waits for the answer as long as the model takes; Stop cancels it.
