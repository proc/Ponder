Engine options

·

Saved options take effect at once: an engine playing now (the game on the board, the Lichess bot’s games) gets them before its next search, and the analysis restarts with them. They are also sent each time the engine starts, tournaments included. Options left at the engine’s default are not sent, except to put back one you reset on an engine that is running.

Settings

Sound

Moves, captures, castling, check, promotion, the start and end of a game, low time and illegal moves. The sounds follow the theme.

Announcer

For games played on the board: yours, an engine’s, a Lichess game. Not tournament games. It takes turns with the AI’s live commentary when both speak; a line that has waited too long is dropped, so it keeps up with the game.

Music

Add a station the address of its stream (MP3, AAC or Ogg) or of a .pls or .m3u playlist, as radio sites and directories such as radio-browser.info give it
Folder

Live classical radio streamed from the internet (or any station you add), or the music files (MP3, AAC, FLAC, Ogg, WAV) in a folder of your own. It plays under the game sounds with its own volume; the note button beside the board, or U, starts and stops it.

AI chat

The knight on f3 is well placed: 12. Nf3 e5 13. dxe5 keeps the pressure.

UCI engines for playing and analysis. Add an engine by choosing its program file. Pick an engine in the list to rename it, set the command line it starts with, edit its options (threads, hash size, …) or remove it.

Turn analysis on with the Analysis button in the toolbar. It follows the position on the board, including when you step back through the moves or browse games from a file, and uses its own copy of the engine. It pauses while an engine is playing a game.

The AI model used for AI annotate (under Annotate in the Moves tab), the AI panel's chat and the live commentary: from OpenAI, Claude (Anthropic) or Google AI (Gemini) with your API key, or from any OpenAI-compatible server, such as a local one (oMLX, LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp, vLLM).

Nothing is sent until you press AI annotate or send a message in the AI panel: then the game on the board goes to this endpoint, as PGN, with the position shown. It waits for the answer as long as the model takes; Stop cancels it.

Commentary voice

To use it, choose Speech server as the voice in the AI panel's Commentary view, with Read aloud on. Each comment's words are then sent to this endpoint and the sound it answers with is played.

Commentator

Live commentary is turned on in the AI panel's Commentary view. Drag the character anywhere over the window; click it to show or put away its last comment.
